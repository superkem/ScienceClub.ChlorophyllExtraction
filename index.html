Science Club · 40-minute bench protocol

Extract chlorophyll and test how it absorbs light

Grind spinach in ethanol, filter out a green extract, then shine red, green and blue light through it. Your team measures which colours get through and explains why leaves look green.

    Filter the extract

    Remove leaf bits to get a clear green extract. While it drips, prepare the blank tube you will compare it with.

    2AFilter and fill the tubes

      Why a blank? Plain ethanol shows how much light the liquid and glass let through on their own. Comparing with it tells you what the chlorophyll alone absorbs.

      18–33 min · Activity 3

      Light absorbance test
